# Evidence status and decision chronology

The repository preserves the role each result played. A favorable diagnostic
does not become confirmatory after the fact, and a failed competence gate is
not counted as a zero treatment effect.

| Evidence stream | Status | Independent units | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Ouro natural 2Wiki and MuSiQue | held-out confirmation | 124 and 95 pairs | Tests K1 to K3 growth in path control |
| LoopUS-1.7B natural transfer | registered transfer | 93 and 95 pairs | Repeats the endpoint test in another native recurrent family |
| Ouro fictional worlds | prospective confirmation | 288 worlds | Removes factual identity and tests K1 to K4 choice acquisition |
| On/off-path and locality panels | prospective confirmation | 144 held-out worlds | Tests edit specificity and adverse locality |
| LoopUS-8B fictional extension | frozen extension after competence | 288 worlds | Tests whether a competent checkpoint can start in a saturated choice regime |
| Tying by supervision factorial | confirmation after one development seed | 8 new seeds, 600 worlds per seed | Tests which training factor moves control across exits |
| Full LoopUS exits, repair residuals, state access, generation, and longer paths | diagnostic or registered boundary | varies | Interprets scope without changing earlier success rules |

Natural Ouro K1 to K3 was selected using disjoint development behavior before
held-out 2Wiki scoring. The fictional panel, competence rule, K1 to K4
endpoint, and bootstrap were frozen before treatment inference. Structural
confirmation worlds were held apart from execution checks. LoopUS-8B treatment
remained locked until its intact-grammar competence screen passed. The
training factorial used development seed 17 for design decisions, then eight
new paired confirmation seeds for reported intervals.

See `data/frozen_summaries/INDEX.json` for the secondary branches and
`CLAIM_INDEX.md` for the central evidence.
